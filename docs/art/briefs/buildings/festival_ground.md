# Brief: Festival Ground (`festival_ground`)

4 × 4, hosts festivals. No workforce. Harvest Rites.

## Silhouette

Open green + maypole-equivalent (harvest pole) + bunting lines to corner posts.

```
    o--------o
    |\/\/\/\|        bunting strands (ui.accent + paper.highlight)
    |   ||  |        harvest pole centre, 5 m
    | green  |
    o--------o
```

Plan: green centre, pole, 4 corner posts, fire pit E, stalls row S (festival-only
props appear during festivals).

## Materials, tokens

- Ground `field.pasture` mown ring; pole `wood.beam.dark` with harvest crown
  (`field.grain` sheaf); bunting `ui.accent`/`paper.highlight` triangles.
- Signature props: **harvest pole + bunting** + festival stalls (appear only while
  a festival runs — the running read).

## Island-zoom read

Empty green square most of the year (deliberate calm); bunting + stalls during
festivals. The contrast IS the read.

## States

- Festival running: stalls up, bunting strung, fire lit, lantern posts.
- Dormant: pole + corner posts only, green empty.
- Paused N/A (no workforce; festivals pause via command, visuals follow the event).
- Construction: posts first, then ground ring at 60%.

## Upgrade hook (M2-30)

None; later eras dress the same ground (bunting colours per festival, data-driven).
