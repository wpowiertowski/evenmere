# Brief: Stone Storehouse (`stone_storehouse`)

Upgrade variant of `storehouse` (M2-30 owns content: 4 stone, Masonry).
See `storehouse.md` for the shared silhouette and states. Differences only:

- Walls `stone.mid` with `stone.light` quoins; footing one course higher.
- Roof `roof.shingle` unchanged — silhouette identical, material greyer.
- Carts 3: a second hand cart at the dock is the working-state tell.
- The kit builds this by swapping the wall part on the storehouse spec: no new
  silhouette, no new props. This is the M2-30 upgrade-path proof case.
