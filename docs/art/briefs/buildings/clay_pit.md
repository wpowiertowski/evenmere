- No roofed building except an open drying lean-to (shed thatch, optional).
- Signature props: **A-frame drying racks with red blanks** + puddling pool
  (`water.river` quad in the pit floor).

## Island-zoom read

Red-brown pit + pale rack lines. The only red ground on the island.

## States

- Idle: racks empty, pool dull.
- Paused: standard — tarp over racks.
- Construction: pit steps cut first (terrain decal), racks at 60%.
