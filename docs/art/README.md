# Evenmere art bible — coverage

Every Era I building, good and tier in `progression.md`, with its brief and the
task that will build it: **ART-02** (painted PNG icon) or **ART-03** (code-built mesh).
Era II rows are marked *later* — briefs for them are written after ART-01 from
these templates; no ART-02/03 asset may start them.

Docs: `style-guide.md` (binding numbers) · `palette.md` (68 tokens) ·
`briefs/icons.md` · `briefs/terrain.md` · `briefs/buildings/<id>.md`.
