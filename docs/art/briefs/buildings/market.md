3 × 3, coverage 16. 2 Settlers. Start building.

## Silhouette

Open-sided stall hall: low shed roofs around three sides, striped awnings.


Plan: hall core 2 × 2 N, awning stalls on S and E, goods rows.

## Roof, materials, tokens

- Posts `wood.beam.dark`, shed roofs thatch 30° (low pitch = market-only).
- Awnings alternate `roof.canvas` / `pottery.glaze` stripes.
- Signature props: **stall tables with goods dots** (bread loaves, fish, pots —
  use `good.*` fills at street zoom) + hanging scale beam.

## Island-zoom read

Striped awning band. The only stripes on the island; visible through trees.

## States

- Working: awnings out, tables stocked (colour dots), bunting on feast days.
- Idle: awnings half-rolled, tables bare.
- Paused: awnings fully rolled + standard tarp over the hall.
- Construction: standard scaffold; awning poles at 70%.
