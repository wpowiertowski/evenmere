## Roof, materials, tokens

- Cottage wattle + thatch 45°. Kiln `brick.fired` with `stone.dark` fire mouths.
- Signature props: **bottle kiln stack** + pot rows (amphora miniatures,
  `pottery.glaze`) + clay heap (`clay.raw`).

## Island-zoom read

Cottage with a fat round chimney. The only round tower besides the windmill.

## States

- Working: heat shimmer + smoke from the kiln mouth, pots stacked to fire.
- Idle: kiln cold and dark, pots shelved under the lean-to.
- Paused: standard tarp over cottage door + kiln mouth boarded.
- Construction: standard scaffold; kiln stack at 60%.

## Upgrade hook (M2-30)

None in Era I; Era II keeps the building (pottery stays Settler-made).
